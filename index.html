About Peter Ademuwagun

A creative strategist with a commercial mind

Peter Ademuwagun is a Marketing, Communications and Sales Expert working at the intersection of creativity, strategy, marketing, media, sales and commercial growth. He has worked with notable brands including Payaza Africa, International Workplace Group, Celebration Church Global, Nevon Media HQ, BudCo, and others, taking ideas from strategy to execution and helping build loyal audiences, communities and measurable business outcomes.

His work has contributed to millions of views, thousands in audience growth, and significant financial milestones across different brands, campaigns and platforms. But Peter's work goes beyond the numbers. He has built from scratch, scaled what worked, sold ideas, led teams, and continued to study and develop frameworks that make creative and professional work commercially valuable.

"Creatives and Professionals should not have to choose between meaningful work and meaningful wealth."
